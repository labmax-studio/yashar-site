Privacy Policy — Yashar (ישר)

Effective October 4, 2026

Yashar is a Chrome extension that shows jobs in Israel straight from company career pages. It is operated by Max Lab (Max Zh.) ("we"). This policy explains, in plain words, what is collected, why, and where.

In short

  • No sign-up. We never ask for your name, email, phone or CV.
  • The extension does not read the websites you visit or your browsing history — it has no permission to.
  • We do not sell data or use it for advertising.
  • Data of an inactive user is deleted automatically after 60 days.

Stored only in your browser

  • Your filters (job-title search, city, "Israel" / "Israel + remote") and whether notifications are on.
  • Daily-digest state and the new-jobs counter on the icon.
  • The anonymous sign-in key (see below).

This is not sent to us for storage. When jobs are loaded, filters are sent only as part of the request and are not stored on the server. Uninstalling the extension removes all local data.

Stored on our server

Data Why
Anonymous ID — a random number created at install, not linked to your identity So the extension remembers what you have already seen
Country and interface language To show the right jobs and language
Jobs you viewed (which job and when) To mark them "viewed" in the feed
Last activity date To delete inactive users
Company suggestions you send (link and optional comment) So we can review and add the company; you see the status under "My suggestions"
Job clicks — which job, where in the extension, when. Without your ID Aggregate statistics: which companies and jobs are of interest

IP address: like any internet service, our infrastructure provider processes your IP address to deliver requests and to limit abuse (e.g. mass creation of users). We do not store IP addresses in our tables.

Who we share data with

  • Supabase — our database and server provider. Servers are in Frankfurt, Germany (EU).
  • Employers — clicking a job opens the company's own site or hiring system, where their policy applies. The link carries a tag saying the visit came from Yashar (UTM) — nothing about you.
  • Nobody else, unless required by law.

Retention

  • An anonymous ID with no activity for 60 days is deleted automatically, together with the jobs you viewed.
  • Company suggestions are kept after that, but without your ID — as a record about the company only.
  • Clicks are stored without an ID in the first place.

Your rights

Under the Israeli Privacy Protection Law you may ask to access, correct or delete your data. You can delete your data yourself at any time: "Delete my data" at the bottom of the extension. Your anonymous ID and everything linked to it is removed from the server immediately (company suggestions stay without the ID), and the extension starts fresh with a new ID. Questions: labmax.information@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top. Material changes will also be shown in the extension.

How we count

  • Source: only the official career pages of the 73 companies in Yashar — not the whole market.
  • Profession: by job title, approximately. Anything not recognised is “Other”. “General applications” (talent pools, “Don’t see your role?”) are a separate row, without a median.
  • Job age: by the employer’s publish date, where it is known (92% of jobs). Median and reopened share only with at least 10 dated jobs from at least 5 companies; otherwise “Not enough data”.
  • The median is by company: first each company’s median, then the median of those — so one company with many jobs does not set the picture.
  • A job in several cities counts in each. One snapshot a day.
